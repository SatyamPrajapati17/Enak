HOW TO INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Transfer "enak-release.apk" to your phone via USB cable, Google Drive,
   or WhatsApp/Telegram.
2. Open your phone's File Manager and tap "enak-release.apk".
3. When prompted, toggle "Allow from this source" / "Install unknown apps".
4. Tap "Install".

------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO RESOLVE GOOGLE PLAY PROTECT ("App blocked to protect your device"):
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why this happens:
Enak includes powerful system-level digital wellbeing features:
- Usage Stats (tracks real app minutes)
- Accessibility Service (instant app blocker)
- Device Administrator (Strict Mode to prevent bypass)
Because this APK is sideloaded directly rather than downloaded from the
Google Play Store, Google Play Protect flags it as a sensitive app.

HOW TO BYPASS:
Method A (Direct Bypass):
1. On the Play Protect popup, look for "More details" (small arrow/link).
2. Tap "More details".
3. Tap "Install anyway".

Method B (If "Install anyway" is hidden):
1. Open the "Google Play Store" app on your phone.
2. Tap your Profile icon in the top right corner.
3. Tap "Play Protect".
4. Tap the Settings Gear icon (⚙️) in the top right.
5. Temporarily toggle off "Scan apps with Play Protect".
6. Return to your File Manager and install "enak-release.apk".
7. (Optional) Turn Play Protect scanning back on after installation.

FIRST LAUNCH & PERMISSIONS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
On first launch, Enak starts with an interactive onboarding questionnaire:
1. What should your mindful coach call you? (Name input)
2. Your mindful intention (e.g. Reduce doomscrolling, Deep study focus)
3. Coach persona (Calm Zen, Direct Strict, Friendly, Philosophical Monk)
4. Sequential Permissions Setup:
   - Usage Access (tracks daily app screen time)
   - Display Over Other Apps (shows mindful pause screen when limits are hit)
   - Unrestricted Battery (prevents Android Doze from killing background timers)
   - Accessibility Service (instant app blocking)
   - Device Admin (Strict Mode prevention)

Enjoy mindful, intentional screen time!
